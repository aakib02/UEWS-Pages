UEWS Account Deletion Request

Unique Education and Welfare Society (UEWS) respects user privacy and provides users with the ability to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your UEWS account, please submit a request through the Help & Support section available within the UEWS mobile application.

If you are unable to access the application, you may contact the UEWS support team and provide your registered email address or membership details for verification.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Profile Information
  • Membership Information
  • Account Access Information

Data Retention

Certain records may be retained for a limited period where required by applicable laws, regulatory obligations, fraud prevention requirements, or operational needs.

Once verification is completed, eligible account data will be deleted within a reasonable period.

Support

For any questions regarding account deletion, please use the Help & Support section within the UEWS application.
