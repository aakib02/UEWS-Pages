UEWS Child Safety Standards Policy

Unique Education and Welfare Society (UEWS) is committed to maintaining a safe, secure, and respectful environment for all users, including children and minors. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), child grooming, harassment, abuse, exploitation, or any activity that may endanger children.

Our Commitment

  • UEWS strictly prohibits content, behavior, or activities that exploit, abuse, or harm children.
  • Any user found engaging in CSAE-related activities may have their account suspended or permanently removed.
  • We cooperate with applicable law enforcement agencies and child protection authorities when legally required.
  • We take appropriate action against content or conduct that violates child safety standards.

User Responsibilities

Users must not upload, share, distribute, request, promote, or engage with content that exploits, harms, or endangers children. All users are expected to comply with applicable laws and community standards while using UEWS.

Reporting and Enforcement

UEWS reserves the right to investigate reports of child safety violations and take appropriate action, including account suspension, content removal, and reporting to relevant authorities where required by law.

Compliance

UEWS complies with applicable child protection laws and continuously reviews its policies and practices to help ensure a safe environment for all users.
