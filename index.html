Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering an account, updating your profile data, or executing welfare donations. This includes your full name, authenticated email address, phone number, date of birth, blood group context, and address details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is securely utilized to manage institutional society memberships, process transparent audited donations, distribute automated push alerts for announcements, and synchronize secure local biometric login capabilities. We do not sell or trade your data to third-party data handlers.

3. Donation and Payment Information

When users make donations through the application, payment processing is handled by secure third-party payment service providers. We do not store complete card details, banking credentials, or sensitive payment information on our servers. Payment data is processed securely in accordance with applicable security standards.

4. Device Permissions & Security

Our system requests local storage permissions strictly for compiling and storing monthly financial statements or ledgers directly on your device. Biometric access (fingerprint/Face ID) parameters remain strictly encrypted inside your device hardware keychain matrix and are never transmitted to our backend microservices.

5. Data Retention & Erasure

We retain your information as long as your user account remains active inside the Welfare Society dashboard stack. Users maintain full rights to request profile restriction or absolute content erasure by initiating communication directly through the Help & Support module.

Account Deletion Request Notice: Users may submit a formal deletion request through the Help & Support section available within the application. Upon verification, eligible account information will be permanently deleted within a reasonable period, subject to statutory legal and operational compliance requirements.

6. Compliance and Queries

This document is formulated in complete alignment with global security data guidelines and Google Play Console privacy rules. For legal queries, feel free to contact our administrative welfare cluster engineers.
